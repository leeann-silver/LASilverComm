# Site change log

A running record of changes to lasilvercommunications.com: what changed, where and why. Newest first.

## 2026-10-07: Service tier names (index.html, faq.html, record-framework.html)

**Why:** Settled the three tiers as RECORD Audit, Build the Record and Category Ownership, so the site matches internal documents and the audit is named for the framework rather than for "authority."

**What changed:**

- "Authority Audit" renamed "RECORD Audit" everywhere it appeared: service cards, pricing FAQ, contact form option, page calls to action and structured data.
- Third tier renamed from "Become the Source" to "Category Ownership" (service card, pricing FAQ, contact form option, structured data). The "Build the Record. Become the Source." tagline is unchanged.
- "Build The Record" standardized to "Build the Record."
- The audit description no longer lists six things that didn't match the framework (Findability, Expertise, Corroboration, Recognition, Documentation, AI visibility). It now says the audit scores all six RECORD layers, shows what AI says and what evidence it relies on, and delivers a roadmap.

## 2026-10-07: Layer refinements (record-framework.html)

- Recognition definition now leads with AI citations, on the page and in the structured data, matching how the scorecard measures the layer.
- Owned "What good looks like" rewritten in plain English: "Pages AI can read easily, with clear definitions near the top and FAQ answers that stand on their own."

## 2026-10-07: RECORD layer definitions, v1.2 (record-framework.html)

**Why:** Repositioning LASilver as a modern PR firm that builds the public record, with AI as the reason the landscape changed rather than the product. The layer definitions are updated so the framework page, the homepage and the Client RECORD Scorecard (Layer Definitions tab) all say the same thing. The old Record and Owned definitions overlapped.

**What changed:**

- Hero: replaced "RECORD is the build methodology behind authority in the AI era" with a one-sentence declarative definition: "The RECORD Framework is a six-layer PR system for building the public evidence behind a company's expertise, reputation and recognition."
- Six layer cards: new headline and definition for each layer, plus a "What good looks like" line taken from the scorecard.
  - Record: Build the body of work.
  - Expertise: Have something worth citing.
  - Corroboration: Let others prove it.
  - Owned: Give people a source to reference.
  - Recognition: Earn recognition beyond your own channels.
  - Documentation: Make the evidence clear and findable.
- Layer names, behaviors (Publish, Think, Validate, Own, Earn, Structure) and asset tags unchanged.
- Added a visible version and date: Version 1.2, updated October 7, 2026.
- Structured data: DefinedTermSet and all six DefinedTerm descriptions rewritten to match; dateModified moved from 2026-06-27 to 2026-10-07.
